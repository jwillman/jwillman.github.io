Pinkka – Privacy

Pinkka is a private application used only by its owner to view their own account information. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data is processed

Only the owner's own account details, balances and transactions, retrieved with the owner's explicit consent through a regulated account information service.

How it is stored

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner. It is not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke account access consent at any time and delete the stored data.
